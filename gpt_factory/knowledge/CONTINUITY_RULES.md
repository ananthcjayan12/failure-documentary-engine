# Continuity Rules

## Family anchors

Generate one approved anchor for each major family before generating dependent assets. The anchor establishes:

- subject identity,
- color science,
- material treatment,
- lighting logic,
- realism level,
- era and location cues.

## Preserve across related assets

- aircraft or vehicle geometry,
- livery or paint pattern,
- room architecture and screen arrangement,
- investigator clothing and equipment,
- weather family and time of day,
- recurring evidence shape and damage pattern.

## Deliberate variation

Change camera distance, angle, crop, depth, environmental action, and narrative emphasis. Do not create variety by mutating the recurring subject.

## Reuse spacing

The same full-frame composition should normally appear only once. Secondary uses should employ detail crops, overlays, freeze frames, silhouettes, or background treatment.
