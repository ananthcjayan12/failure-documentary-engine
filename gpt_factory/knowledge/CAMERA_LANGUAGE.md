# Documentary Camera Language

## Establishing frames

Use wide 24–35 mm-equivalent compositions with strong geography, scale, and negative space.

## Technical evidence

Use stable 50–85 mm-equivalent framing, restrained depth of field, and clearly separated subject details.

## Human or control-room scenes

Prefer anonymous silhouettes, hands, equipment, reflections, and over-the-shoulder views. Avoid fabricated identifiable faces unless supplied and explicitly required.

## Atmospheric plates

Use locked or nearly locked cameras. Movement should come from clouds, rain, ocean, mist, instrument glow, dust, or particles.

## Reuse-aware composition

A master asset should offer:

1. one strong wide frame,
2. one useful close crop,
3. one clean background area for overlays,
4. a stable opening and ending region for animation.

Keep important subjects within the central 80% safe area to survive local 16:9 cropping.
