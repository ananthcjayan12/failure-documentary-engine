# Failure Investigation Channel — Visual Style Bible

## Visual promise

Every frame should feel like a restrained, premium investigation documentary: intelligent, technically credible, cinematic, and respectful of real people.

## Core palette

- Deep navy and near-black foundations
- Charcoal, slate, and steel-blue materials
- Restrained teal/cyan instrumentation
- Small amber practical highlights
- Red only for danger paths, warnings, or confirmed critical events

## Image character

- Photorealistic reconstruction rather than sensational poster art
- Real-world scale, gravity, weather, materials, and lighting
- Fine natural grain and atmospheric depth
- Controlled contrast with readable shadow detail
- Negative space reserved for later maps, labels, and evidence graphics

## Avoid

- Fireballs unless confirmed and narratively necessary
- Heroic action-film framing
- Futuristic holograms
- Oversaturated teal-and-orange grading
- Visible suffering or exploitative imagery
- Text embedded in generated images
- Famous television-show branding or copied title treatments
