# Aircraft and Vehicle Realism Rules

## Aircraft

- Preserve the correct number and placement of engines.
- Preserve plausible wing sweep, tail geometry, landing gear, windows, doors, and navigation lights.
- Avoid warped fuselages, merged wings, duplicated stabilizers, or impossible control surfaces.
- Aircraft attitude must match flight phase: taxi, takeoff, climb, cruise, bank, descent, or search pattern.
- Use restrained camera distance to hide unimportant micro-details while keeping silhouette accurate.
- Do not invent airline text or logos. A generic period-appropriate livery is preferable when exact branding is not required.

## Ships and underwater vehicles

- Keep hull, mast, propulsor, sensor, and light placement consistent across recurring shots.
- Search vessels should look operational, not like military science fiction.
- Underwater vehicles should move slowly and remain stable in deep water.

## Infrastructure and machinery

- Preserve load paths, structural proportions, connection logic, and realistic materials.
- Do not use impossible sparks, exposed energy fields, or decorative machinery without function.
